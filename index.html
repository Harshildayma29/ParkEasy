BEHIND THE PROJECT

Our design thinking process

1. Empathize

Ask drivers about parking problems and wasted time.

2. Define

People do not know where spaces are free before arriving.

3. Ideate

Design search, availability details, and booking features.

4. Prototype

Make a website using HTML, CSS, JS, Python, and SQLite.

5. Test

Try bookings and cancellations; ask classmates for feedback.
